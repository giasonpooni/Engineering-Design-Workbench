# Scope

The first slice answers one question:

> Given a proposer loop, a department budget, and declared observations,
> may the next token burst be spent — or must the host compose, query a
> plant, or refuse?

It does **not** claim to be a prompt optimizer, a router, or a judge
model.

## In scope

- Department budgets on `ledger`, `evidence`, `exploration`, `gauge`.
- Letters `ADMIT`, `COMPOSE`, `REQUEST_EVIDENCE`, `REINDEX`, `CLOSE_BUDGET`.
- Spill codes: `GAUGE_SPILL`, `RANK_FLAT`, `H1_NO_GLUE`,
  `COMPOSE_INSTEAD`, `DEPARTMENT_STARVED`, `BUDGET_CLOSED`,
  `META_PRECISION_UNSTABLE`, `YIELD_BELOW_THRESHOLD`.
- A composition store of caller-declared morphisms.
- Receipts other kernels may cite. Receipts do not ACCEPT a world.

## Not implemented

- Embeddings, wording charts, or a second LLM as the yield observer.
- Imports of `gat`, `lyapunov`, or `sensitivity`.
- Learned routers, FrugalGPT cascades, grammar-constrained decoding.
- Energy-per-token physics as the objective.
- A Lyapunov certificate on the budget dynamics (that would be a
  PLSR plant, not this kernel).

Exploration may hold entropy. Gauge may be declared and is refused
as spend. Ship fibers should set exploration slack explicitly; this
package does not infer a ship/explore regime from adjectives.
