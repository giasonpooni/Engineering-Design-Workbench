## Implemented scope

- Strict versioned scientific specification compiler for coupled thermal,
  axial thermoelastic and synthetic observation reference models.
- Local and bounded subprocess execution, OCI adapter and packaging, distinct
  numerical verification and declared spatial reduction comparisons.
- Retained specification source identities, exact live/restore bindings and
  correction propagation through dependent operations and claims.
- Fixed-mesh timestep studies at common sample times including startup, with
  bounded analytic reference, explicit unassessed samples and empirical error
  changes.
- Compact inspection and synchronized standalone retained-state review.

Operation results, executions, source evidence, numerical reports and physical
authority remain distinct. Physical validation is `not_assessed`; canonical
admission is false. Sensors and material constants are illustrative. A sealed
record proves local consistency, not independent attestation.
