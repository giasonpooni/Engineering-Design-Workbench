# Original asset permission policy

Policy version 1.1 · 2 October 2026

**Enrollment policy established; no NET asset version is enrolled.**
The [rights register](rights-register.json) is empty. Publishing this policy
does not restrict an existing NET file or change its AGPL software grant.
The policy and templates are documentation under applicable repository terms.

## Declared owner and reference boundary

The project owner stated that assets are original and the corpus serves as
reference material, and expressly identified **Notation Systems Inc.** as
the legal asset owner on 2 October 2026. This is an owner-supplied declaration,
not an independently verified assignment, corporate-status check or worldwide
legal clearance. It does not itself transfer rights.

The owner identified the current group on 3 October 2026 as Notation Systems
Inc. as parent, with Notations Gaming, Notation Manufacturing and Notations
Laboratories as child companies. Notation Systems Inc. remains the declared
rights holder for project-owned original material. Each notice and signed grant
must identify the actual legal licensor, its rights and its authorized signatory.
A child-company name or group relationship alone does not confer licensing
authority or transfer rights. This records the owner's declaration without
certifying incorporation or title. Existing creator credits and third-party
rights are preserved.

Reference images, recordings, publications, scans, source prose and other
external expression retain their own rights. Research, possession, links,
hashes and inspiration do not transfer them. Original annotations and qualifying
original selection/arrangement may have separate rights; facts, ideas, methods
and historical events themselves are not claimed. Copying protected source
expression into an output requires a recorded lawful basis. Running NET does
not assign rights in its inputs or outputs.

## Enrollment and controlling terms

An asset is covered only when its exact version is registered with status
'enrolled' and an applicable notice accompanies it. Record paths and hashes,
creation provenance, actual owner/licensing authority, prior grants, incorporated
sources, distribution obligations and contact before enrollment. Confirm
compatibility with the actual combined distribution. Unresolved rights block
controlled delivery.

No existing NET code, fixtures, examples, documentation or third-party component
is enrolled here. New independent artwork, models, audio, animation, narrative
and other non-software assets may be enrolled where eligible. Prior valid grants
survive; a new hash does not erase them. New software terms need their own
explicit scope and rights review.

## Notice for an enrolled asset

Copyright (c) 2026 Notation Systems Inc., solely in original protected material
it owns or is authorized to license in the enrolled version. All rights reserved
except valid existing grants and independently applicable law/platform rights.

No public licence is granted for enrolled material. Before an act requiring
the Rights Holder's authorization, request permission and obtain an express
signed written grant identifying version, recipient, permitted acts, purpose,
users, systems, territory and term. This includes copying, installation or
execution where applicable, adaptation, publication, distribution, extraction,
hosting, incorporation and commercial or noncommercial reuse, within its
enforceable rights.

To the fullest extent permitted by law, reproduction/extraction rights are
expressly reserved for text and data mining, datasets, embeddings, AI training,
fine tuning, evaluation, distillation and improvement. A grant must expressly
identify these acts where permission is required. Mandatory exceptions, valid
prior grants and provider/platform permissions survive. This policy is not a
deployed machine-readable access control.

Requests: [licensing request](https://github.com/giasonpooni/Notations-Systems-Terminal/issues/new?template=licensing-request.yml&title=Licensing%20request).
Request, acknowledgement, payment, credit, access or silence is not permission.
Do not upload assets or confidential information to a public issue; request
a private discussion before disclosing sensitive terms.

## Agreements and delivery

Complete the [business agreement and schedules](templates/PERMISSION_ONLY_AGREEMENT.md)
before controlled business access, including owner details, signatories,
addresses, versions and necessary installation/execution/copying rights.
The template remains unexecuted. Consumer player distribution requires an
appropriate agreement specifying the player's permissions.

Public hosting preserves platform permissions and cannot require prior
permission for every act, including applicable platform-authorized AI uses.
Use controlled delivery after an executed agreement
where strict prior-permission access is required. This policy creates no assent
by browsing, secrecy for public files, automatic penalty, patent/trademark grant
or jurisdiction agreement. Enforcement depends on applicable rights, facts,
mandatory law and competent courts, without universal certification.
