## Business Access and Permission Agreement

Complete Schedules A and B and execute this Agreement before supplying controlled Materials. This form is intended for business evaluation or commercial access. Consumer game distribution requires an appropriate consumer agreement.

### 1 Parties and defined materials

This Agreement is between the Rights Holder identified in Schedule B and the Recipient identified there. Each additional Rights Holder must sign or be represented by an expressly authorized agent. A related company, studio or brand is not a party solely because its name appears in this framework.

“Materials” means only the software, source or object code, documents, designs, models, datasets, audiovisual works, game assets or other items identified by version in Schedule A and incorporated into Schedule B. Restrictions apply to intellectual property rights owned or validly controlled by the Rights Holder and to lawful contractual obligations concerning controlled information. Third party materials and existing grants are identified separately.

### 2 No permission before an effective grant

Neither this Agreement without a completed grant nor availability, delivery, possession, access credentials, discussion, attribution, acknowledgement, payment or silence authorizes an activity. Permission becomes effective only when both parties execute Schedule B, incorporating this Agreement by version, and satisfy its stated conditions precedent. Recipient must not access controlled Materials before that effective time.

### 3 Scope of permission

The Rights Holder grants only the acts expressly listed in Schedule B, for its named Recipient, purpose, users, systems, locations, territory and term. The grant is nonexclusive, nontransferable and nonsublicensable unless Schedule B expressly provides otherwise. Each stated scope limitation defines the permission granted. An uncompleted scope field confers no authorization for that category and must be resolved before access begins. Specify any necessary installation, execution, temporary copying and backup permissions.

Affiliates, contractors, cloud providers, AI services and agents receive no independent permission. Recipient may involve them only as expressly authorized in Schedule B and remains responsible for their compliance. Any extension, new release, new workload, different deployment or additional purpose requires a further signed grant. An evaluation grant does not authorize production or external service use.

### 4 Restrictions beyond the grant

Except as expressly authorized, Recipient shall not reproduce, execute, modify, adapt, translate, distribute, sublicense, sell, publish, display, perform, host, provide network access to, or incorporate Materials into another product or service. Recipient shall not reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, defeat access controls, remove rights notices or misrepresent origin, except to the extent applicable law does not permit such restrictions. Restrictions on uses outside copyright arise from this assented Agreement, subject to mandatory law.

## Controls Ownership and Confidentiality

### 5 AI data mining and external processing

Except as separately specified in Schedule B, Recipient shall not submit Materials to an AI system or external processing service, use them to build datasets, embeddings or retrieval indexes, or reproduce or extract them for text and data mining, model training, fine tuning, distillation, evaluation or improvement. An approved AI or processing use must identify the provider, system, activity, retention, access, training policy and outputs. Access for one purpose does not authorize another. Mandatory statutory exceptions remain subject to section 12.

### 6 Ownership notices and outputs

The Rights Holder retains its existing rights. This Agreement does not transfer ownership or grant a patent or trademark licence unless Schedule B expressly identifies that grant. Recipient shall preserve applicable authorship, copyright, provenance and third party notices on authorized copies. Recipient shall not imply endorsement or use protected branding beyond any express grant or independently lawful use.

Recipient retains its preexisting rights. Ownership, disclosure and reuse of modifications and outputs must be stated in Schedule B; no automatic assignment of all Recipient work is created here. Outputs containing protected Materials remain subject to the applicable rights. This Agreement does not create copyright ownership of otherwise unprotected factual results. No contribution or feedback assignment is implied; any transfer requires a separate executed provision.

### 7 Confidentiality and safeguarding

“Confidential Information” means nonpublic Materials and nonpublic information disclosed under this Agreement that is marked confidential or that a reasonable business recipient would understand to be confidential from its nature and disclosure circumstances. Public availability is not converted into secrecy by a label.

Recipient shall use Confidential Information solely for the authorized purpose, restrict disclosure to specifically authorized persons with a need to know and written obligations at least as protective, and maintain reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. Recipient shall not upload Confidential Information to an unauthorized repository, collaboration service, AI provider or other external system. Recipient shall promptly notify the Rights Holder of suspected unauthorized access, use or disclosure, take reasonable containment steps and preserve relevant evidence.

These duties exclude information Recipient can substantiate was lawfully known without a confidentiality duty, independently developed without use of Confidential Information, lawfully obtained from another source without such a duty, or publicly available without breach. Legally compelled disclosure is permitted only to the required extent, with advance notice and reasonable cooperation where lawful. Trade secret duties continue while the information remains a trade secret under applicable law. Other confidentiality duties continue for five years after termination unless Schedule B specifies a different lawful period.

### 8 Records and bounded verification

Recipient shall maintain records reasonably sufficient to verify the authorized versions, users, deployments and activities. On reasonable notice the Rights Holder may request relevant compliance records or an officer’s certification. Any on-site or system inspection requires a separately agreed scope or lawful order. Verification shall protect unrelated confidential information and personal data. This clause grants no unrestricted access to Recipient’s systems.

## Termination Remedies and Legal Terms

### 9 Suspension termination and retained evidence

Permission ends at expiry or as specified in Schedule B. The Rights Holder may suspend access immediately where reasonably necessary to address an actual or credibly suspected scope, confidentiality or security breach. It may terminate for a material breach by written notice if the breach is incapable of cure, or remains uncured ten business days after notice specifying the breach.

After expiry or termination, Recipient shall cease the affected activities and within ten business days return or securely delete controlled copies and certify compliance on request. Copies required by law or a genuine legal hold may be retained in restricted archival custody without operational use. Inaccessible routine backups may remain until ordinary deletion, subject to continuing restrictions. Neither party shall destroy evidence required to be preserved by law. Licences granted separately from this Agreement remain governed by their own terms and are not terminated by its termination. Ownership, confidentiality, accrued obligations, remedies and necessary verification and legal provisions survive; operational permission ends.

### 10 Remedies and payment

For unauthorized acts within its enforceable rights, the Rights Holder may seek injunctive relief, damages, an accounting of profits where available, delivery up and court-awarded costs. Availability and amount remain for the competent court under applicable law. Payment or settlement authorizes an activity only if an executed writing expressly provides that authorization.

Recipient is responsible for validating its intended deployment. Any support, warranty, indemnity or liability allocation must be expressly recorded.

This Agreement does not revoke or reduce a valid earlier licence, impose forbidden further restrictions on third party licensed materials, or claim exclusive copyright over ideas, methods, facts, mathematical concepts or independent implementations. No additional permission is implied. An applicable statutory exception extends only to its own conditions and scope.

### 14 Complete agreement and changes

This Agreement and executed schedules constitute the complete agreement for the grant. Schedule B overrides a provision only by identifying it and its replacement. Amendments, extensions and waivers require an authorized signed writing. Enforcement delay alone is not waiver. Invalid provisions are severed only as legally permitted. Counterparts and valid electronic signatures are permitted. Notices use Schedule B addresses. Later website updates do not amend an existing grant.

## Adoption and Enforcement Preparation

Use a default refusal to grant rights, an executed version-specific authorization and controlled delivery together. This is the proposed implementation approach; local enforceability depends on the work, rights, recipient, distribution method and applicable law.

### Establish ownership before restrictive release

Verify the precise legal owner for each work. Obtain appropriate signed contractor and contributor assignments or licensing authority; address moral rights separately where relevant. Company names, payment, account control and repository administration do not alone prove copyright ownership. Complete the register and licensing contact before publication. [1]

### Protect earlier licences and upstream obligations

Preserve earlier valid releases and permissions. GPLv3 and AGPLv3 grants are irrevocable while their conditions are met and prohibit forbidden further restrictions; covered combined distributions can carry copyleft obligations. MPL covered source retains its own terms. MIT materials retain required notices. New eligible wholly owned releases can use different terms only where all relevant rights and obligations permit. A monorepo does not merge these licences or establish a right to restrict upstream code. This draft does not certify any repository’s eligibility. [6–9]

### Choose a controlled distribution method

Keep unpublished source, private evidence and licensed assets behind access controls. Execute the grant before issuing credentials or deliveries. Record the accepted agreement version, Recipient identity, asset hashes and delivery history. Review service-provider terms and approve processors expressly. Current GitHub Terms provide public repository users service-based viewing and forking permissions and grant GitHub and affiliates specified processing and AI rights. A notice in a public repository does not erase those grants. [10]

### Keep confidentiality factual

Use confidentiality agreements, restricted access and reasonable security measures for actual nonpublic information. Public code cannot be made secret merely by adding a confidential label. Treat independently developed and lawfully acquired information according to the Agreement’s exclusions. [4]

### Reserve mining rights appropriately

For eligible public online content, implement and verify an appropriate machine-readable reservation in addition to the readable notice where applicable. EU DSM Article 4 permits express reservations, including machine-readable means online; Article 3 preserves qualifying scientific research mining, and Article 7 makes contrary contractual terms unenforceable. A robots file or no-AI sentence is not a universal prohibition or a substitute for an appropriate legal and technical implementation. [5]

### Validate the executed form in target markets

Have qualified IP and contract counsel verify the actual chain of title, current distribution obligations, electronic assent, Ontario forum clause and any target-market addenda before external adoption. Berne supports international protection through national treatment, with exceptions and national enforcement rules. Copyright protects expression and qualifying arrangements, not mathematical concepts, underlying facts or historical events themselves. Courts determine the remedies available. The draft therefore makes no promise of universal enforceability or automatic recovery. [2, 3, 11]
