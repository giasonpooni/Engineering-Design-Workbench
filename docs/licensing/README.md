# Permission-only licensing framework

**Status: asset enrollment policy established; zero NET asset versions enrolled. Agreement templates remain unexecuted.**

enrollment for eligible non-software assets. The [register](rights-register.json)
currently has no enrolled NET asset versions. Templates support controlled releases. They do not govern this repository,
replace its existing licence, revoke prior permissions, or restrict rights
granted under existing licences. Repository files remain subject to their
applicable licences and notices. No completed permission grant is issued here.

applicable terms; their model restrictions are not a licence for these files.

NET code covered by its AGPL grant may be used under that grant without separate
outreach or permission. A permission-only notice cannot be overlaid on those
rights. See the [controlling licence](../../LICENSE) and
[component boundaries](../LICENSING.md).

## Requirements before asset enrollment and controlled delivery

For each proposed restricted release, retain the legal rights holder and
authority to license, signed contributor or contractor rights where needed,
per-file and per-component lineage, complete third-party notices and
compatibility analysis, earlier grants, exact included/excluded material and
release hashes, and a verified licensing contact. A new release hash does not
erase earlier rights in unchanged content.

Then select a lawful distribution channel, complete the notice and agreement
schedules, verify that the actual recipient accepts the agreement before
controlled delivery, and assess the terms in intended markets. Obtain qualified
IP and contract counsel's review for the intended adoption. Counsel review is
prudent support for that assessment, not a guarantee of universal enforcement.

Do not remove or narrow existing grants as part of this documentation change.
For wholly owned future additions, different terms remain possible only where
the rights, contribution history, integrations and distribution obligations
permit. Other repositories need their own review; this baseline audit covers NET only.
1792 and Garibaldi carry their own material-specific notices and corpus boundaries.

## International boundaries

Copyright treaties provide international protection through national legal
systems, not one universally controlling enforcement regime. Copyright does
not confer exclusive control over ideas, mathematical concepts, facts or
historical events themselves. Mandatory exceptions, valid existing licences
and third-party rights remain applicable.

Public GitHub hosting carries platform licence grants, including public viewing
and forking permissions. It cannot implement absolute prior-permission access.
For eligible confidential material, use controlled delivery and an executed
agreement, with service-provider permissions reviewed. Appropriate public
machine-readable mining reservations may help where applicable, but cannot
erase mandatory exceptions or independently granted permissions.

The [agreement template's sources](templates/PERMISSION_ONLY_AGREEMENT.md#authoritative-sources)
identify the authorities used for the drafting review, including the WIPO
Copyright Treaty and Berne summary, Canadian Copyright Act, EU DSM Directive,
licence texts and GitHub Terms.
