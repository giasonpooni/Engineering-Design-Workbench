## Organization profiles

### Notation Systems

**Frontier Tooling and Instrumentation for Digital Futures.**

We develop computational instruments and operational tooling that connect scientific methods, specialized computation and human expertise. We build useful tools while studying how measurement, representation, uncertainty and evidence behave when those tools are composed.

Short profile: **Frontier tooling and instrumentation for digital futures. Computational instruments, scientific software and inspectable engineering workflows.**

Accessible infrastructure and a public-interest mission are directions. Do not present nonprofit/charity registration, a foundation, donation eligibility or a legal ownership arrangement as established without confirmation.

### Notations Terminal

**Compose scientific instruments, models and simulations into inspectable engineering workflows.**

A programmable workbench that retains investigation inputs, operations, results and checks while specialist tools keep their implementations. Broader mathematical authoring, agent planning and resource optimization remain capability-specific development work.

### Cartesian Graphics

**Interactive Worlds, Simulation Technology and Digital IP.**

We develop historically grounded games and the graphics, assets, simulation and production technology behind them. 1792 is the primary creative project; Hero of the Two Worlds is secondary. Shared tooling supports the work without replacing artistic direction or merging game state with industrial evidence.

Private creative/IP direction is not a blanket licence claim over dependencies or a completed legal transfer. Existing artifact-specific rights govern reuse.
