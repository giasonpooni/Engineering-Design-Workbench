# Public About copy and review index

Prepared 29 September 2026. **Copy is staged, not applied to GitHub About/account settings or deployed websites.** No legal incorporation, parent/subsidiary relationship, ownership transfer or licence change is asserted by this file.

## Institutional profile

### Cartesian Graphics

**Private Creative, Simulation and Commercial IP Programme.**

Cartesian Graphics is the **intended private ownership/commercialization layer** for proprietary games, simulation products, digital assets, customer-specific systems and other concentrated commercial IP.

**Important:** this is a proposed institutional architecture, not a statement that a legal parent entity, subsidiary structure or completed IP assignment currently exists.

Short profile: **Private creative, simulation and commercial IP programme developing interactive worlds, digital assets and production technology.**

### Notation Systems

**Public-Interest Computational Instrumentation.**

Notation Systems develops accessible computational instruments for representing, measuring, simulating and investigating physical systems. Its programme includes scientific software, research, education, reference implementations, academic collaboration and shared technical infrastructure.

Short profile: **Public-interest computational instrumentation: accessible scientific tools for measurement, representation, simulation, inference and verification.**

Notation Systems is intended to function as a scientific instrumentation commons. Public-interest mission does not imply registered nonprofit/charity status or blanket open licensing.

### Notations Terminal

**A programmable computational instrument for composing scientific tools, models and simulations into inspectable workflows.**

NET is the shared boundary object: an open/public scientific substrate can support universities, industry and Cartesian Graphics while private datasets, workloads, models, worlds, assets and customer configurations remain separately governed.

Short profile: **Programmable computational instrument for composing scientific tools, models and simulations with retained inputs, results and checks.**

## Portfolio boundary

Prefer domain-independent instruments beneath domain-specific workloads:

```text
state estimation · clock sync · frame transform · DSP · graphs
uncertainty · dynamics · conservation · verification · projection
                         ↓
       manufacturing · materials · agriculture · robotics
          energy · GIS · simulation / game worlds
```

PAYLOAD, Caravan, LANDSHARK and TRADEWIND are application consumers of instrumentation rather than definitions of Notation Systems. Their eventual commercial/legal placement remains unresolved.

## Governance classification to do next

Before legal restructuring, classify each repository, dataset, trademark, workload and future research output as:

- **commons** — value increases through broad scientific/engineering use;
- **commercial** — value depends on concentrated ownership;
- **dual-use/shared** — common substrate with separately governed commercial extensions;
- **project-specific** — belongs to a particular game, customer, research project or deployment.

For academic collaboration, separately specify background IP, foreground IP, contributor rights, publication rights, patent rights, trademarks, datasets and commercial-use rights.

## Publication boundaries

These reviews are documentation changes, not merged releases. No runtime source, numerical contract, licence, machine authority, legal ownership or hosting deployment is introduced.

`notation.systems` remains the intended read-only public scientific shell with an explicitly synthetic globe. `notations.io` remains intended hosted Terminal access, not a verified deployment. Domain and corporate naming should be revisited after the governance/IP boundary map, rather than used to imply a legal structure prematurely.

Keep [PUBLIC_POSITIONING.md](PUBLIC_POSITIONING.md) and [RESEARCH_PROGRAMME.md](RESEARCH_PROGRAMME.md) as common editorial references. Instrument READMEs retain precise runnable scope and limits.
